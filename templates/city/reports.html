{{ ui.icon('circle-info') }} How the forecast and recommendations work
  1. Demand is the number of units barangays requested each week (pending, approved and released requests; rejected and cancelled ones are excluded). Weeks with no requests count as zero.
  2. Forecast: each medicine with at least 12 weeks of history and 6 active weeks is modelled with ARIMA(1,1,1) over the last 52 weeks. Others use a 4-week moving average.
  3. Safety stock target = forecast demand × {{ 100 + safety_pct }}%. Restock = target − current city stock (never below zero).
  4. Risk: Critical if stock is below forecast demand, Low if it is below the safety target, Adequate otherwise.
